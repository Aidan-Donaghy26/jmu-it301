In this research I'm using a very niche type of data analysis, called topological data analysis. TDA is basically looking at the shape of the data.

Quick Fact

Topological Data Analysis (TDA) uses tools from topology to study the shape of datasets. It can uncover patterns that traditional statistics might miss.
